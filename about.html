Lesotho in short

  • Lowest point: about 1,400 m
  • Highest point: 3,482 m
  • Snow season: usually June to August
  • Money: the loti (M)

Welcome to the Kingdom in the Sky

Venture into Lesotho is a travel guide built around six places: Khutsong Lodge, Afriski Resort, Metolong Dam, Kome Caves, Sani Pass and Tsehlanyane Park. Each one shows a different side of the country.

Lesotho is called the Kingdom in the Sky because the whole country sits high above sea level. Even its lowest point is higher than the highest point of many countries. The air is thin and clear, the nights are cold and the views go on forever.

Our goal is simple: help you choose a place, see what it costs and send a booking request, all in one website.

Pick Your Season

Seasons in Lesotho
Season Months What to expect
Summer December to February Green hills, warm days and afternoon rain.
Autumn March to May Clear skies and golden grass.
Winter June to August Snow and skiing in the highlands.
Spring September to November Flowers and fewer visitors.

Before You Go

Warm layers, because nights are cold all year. Add sunscreen, sunglasses, sturdy walking shoes and a rain jacket for afternoon storms.

Main roads are fine for normal cars. Mountain roads such as Sani Pass need a 4x4 or a guided tour.

The local money is the loti (M). Carry some cash for small villages and check prices before you travel.

Weather changes fast in the mountains. Check conditions in the morning, tell someone your route and keep a warm layer in your bag.
